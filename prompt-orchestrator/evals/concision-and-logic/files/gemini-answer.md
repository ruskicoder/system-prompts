Great question! Here is a breakdown of the key considerations when choosing a spreading factor.

**Option 1: SF7.** Faster data rate, shorter range.
**Option 2: SF9.** A balanced choice.
**Option 3: SF12.** Longest range, slowest data rate.

Based on our design notes, the team chose SF9 because the 1.2 km gap between Node 3 and Node 4 on the Ta Xua route was not covered at SF7 in the range test on 2 November. Ultimately, choosing the right spreading factor plays a crucial role in ensuring a robust and seamless mesh.
