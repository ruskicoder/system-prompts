## 2.3 Check-in

The Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines a check-in as a message from a hiker tag to the base station. A check-in is sent every 15 min (configurable). A missed check-in raises an alert at the base (which the organiser can silence from the dashboard while a group is resting). Every alert is delivered to the organiser within 5 s. Detectors of AI text are unreliable (Weber-Wulff et al., 2023).
