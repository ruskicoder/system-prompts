# Sprint 4 progress

Here is a breakdown of what the team achieved this sprint.

We tested the relay firmware on 3 nodes along a 400 m trail section. Therefore the TrekLink mesh is ready for the 12 km Ta Xua route. The gateway also uploaded every packet to Firestore within 2 s, so hikers will trust the app more.

Key outcomes:
- Offline check-in queue
- Firebase
- Risk of battery drain on relay nodes
- Pass the capstone defence

This may potentially provide a strong basis for the next sprint. In conclusion, the sprint showed that the mesh works on the trail.
