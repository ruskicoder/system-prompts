# TrekLink register (excerpt)

Team: Do Dang Khoa (leader, firmware), Nguyen Van An (backend), Tran Thi Binh (mobile app), Le Minh Chau (dashboard).

Registered functions:
F1. Hiker registers for an Event with a hiker tag.
F2. Hiker tag sends a check-in every 15 min through the LoRa mesh.
F3. Base station raises an overdue alert 45 min after a missed check-in.
F4. Organiser views every hiker's last check-in on the dashboard map.
F5. Hiker reports a lost hiker from the app.

Scope: a mesh of 3 relay nodes covering a 400 m trail section.
